## Brand & Style

The design system embodies a warm, tactile, and literary sanctum tailored for passionate readers and cultural discourse. It feels like an independent bookshop bathed in late afternoon light—steeped in espresso tones, smooth parchment, and conversational warmth. The atmosphere avoids the sterile chill of conventional social apps, prioritizing focused reflection, slow engagement, and the intimacy of shared reading.

The visual style blends **Literary Warmth** and **Tactile Minimalism** with strict adherence to Arabic RTL (Right-to-Left) reading rhythms:
- Natural paper-like surface hierarchy inspired by cream laid paper and bound cloth covers.
- Subtle warmth over synthetic starkness: zero pure whites (#FFFFFF) or pure blacks (#000000).
- Deliberate literary craftsmanship: quiet borders, generous line spacing, and calligraphic grace paired with contemporary Arabic typographic clarity.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in coffee tones, sun-dappled parchment, and antique gold book spines.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#5A3E2B` - Deep Roast Brown):** Applied to primary actions, key brand moments, prominent active navigation items, and deep emphasis markers.
- **Secondary (`#A47551` - Warm Caramel):** Used for interactive chips, secondary button strokes, progress indicators, and active reading tags.
- **Tertiary (`#C08A3E` - Caramel Gold):** Dedicated to star ratings, bookmarks, reading milestones, and curated editor picks.
- **Surface Canvas (`#F5EEDF` - Warm Linen Beige):** Base background color evoking warm, unbleached book pages.
- **Surface Container (`#FBF7EE` - Light Parchment):** Elevated cards, input fields, sheet modals, and shelf panels.
- **Surface Border (`#D9C7A8` - Muted Tan):** Delicate 1px structural outlines that define surfaces without harshness.
- **Text & Foreground:**
  - High Emphasis: `#2E1F16` (Dark Espresso) for titles, headings, and high-priority content.
  - Medium Emphasis: `#7A6250` (Soft Brown) for secondary labels, timestamps, metadata, and author subtitles.
  - Low Emphasis: `#A89885` (Muted Hazel) for placeholders and subtle structural dividers.

## Typography

Typographic scale prioritizes legibility in Arabic naskh proportions while pairing seamlessly with literary Latin flourishes.

### Typographic Principles
- **Body & Interface Text:** Built on modern, open, and well-balanced Arabic typographic forms (via IBM Plex Sans Arabic). It offers clear character distinction, ample counter spaces, and natural Arabic diacritic placement.
- **Editorial Headlines & Literary Quotes:** Noto Serif provides an elevated, classic presence suited for long quotes, author excerpts, and chapter headings.
- **Display Accents & Masthead:** Decorative Latin touches (such as club insignias or edition monograms) use delicate calligraphic scripts (Ballet), while core Arabic headings preserve authoritative and readable naskh aesthetics.
- **RTL Baseline Rhythms:** Arabic script demands ~20-30% more vertical headroom than Latin glyphs. Line-heights are generously set (24px on 15px body) to eliminate glyph clipping and ease sustained reading.

## Layout & Spacing

A mobile-first fluid layout architected around comfortable hand postures, horizontal card reels, and natural RTL scanning.

### Layout Philosophy
- **RTL Fluid Geometry:** Directionality flows strictly right-to-left. Primary navigational icons, back chevrons (pointing right `→`), list metadata, and avatar arrangements mirror standard LTR structures.
- **Canvas Margins:**
  - Mobile: `1.25rem` (20px) outer edge margins protect text from thumb interference.
  - Tablet/Desktop: Constrained max-width layout (centered at 720px for reading feeds, 1080px for discovery shelves) with `2rem` (32px) margins.
- **Component Rhythms:**
  - Card horizontal carousels bleed cleanly to the left screen edge with trailing edge padding.
  - Inter-card stack distance locks to `space-md` (16px).
  - Internal card padding uses `space-md` to `space-lg` (16px-24px) to retain an uncluttered, book-page feel.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is tactile rather than synthetic. The system relies on tonal paper layering, subtle tan outlines, and warm, diffused brown shadows rather than high-contrast drops.

### Elevation Hierarchy
1. **Canvas Level (0dp):** `#F5EEDF` (Warm Beige). The ground floor representing the study desk or book table.
2. **Sheet / Card Level (1dp):** `#FBF7EE` (Lighter Cream) paired with a 1px border of `#D9C7A8` and a warm ambient shadow:
   `box-shadow: 0 2px 8px -2px rgba(46, 31, 22, 0.05), 0 1px 3px -1px rgba(46, 31, 22, 0.04);`
3. **Floating Shelves & App Bars (2dp):** Surface `#FBF7EE` with a slightly deeper warm contact blur:
   `box-shadow: 0 4px 16px -4px rgba(46, 31, 22, 0.08), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(46, 31, 22, 0.04);`
4. **Modals & Drawers (3dp):** Elevated reading menus, comment drawers, and bottom action sheets:
   `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -6px rgba(46, 31, 22, 0.14);`
   Paired with a tinted backdrop overlay: `rgba(46, 31, 22, 0.35)`.

## Shapes

The shape system evokes warm, well-bound physical objects with hand-friendly rounded contours.

### Shape Scales
- **Cards & Reading Panels:** 16px (`1rem`) border-radius. Mimics curved book corners and smooth paper cardstock.
- **Buttons, Inputs & Form Elements:** 12px (`0.75rem`) border-radius. Provides an organic, comfortable touch target.
- **Chips, Badges & Book Format Tags:** 8px to fully pill-shaped (for status indicators and reading categories).
- **Book Cover Covers:** Subtle 4px radius on the open edge and a soft 1px radius on the spine edge to mimic bound cloth spines.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled `#5A3E2B` background with `#FBF7EE` text, 12px border radius, 48px height for touch ergonomics. Active state deepens to `#422D1F`.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Background transparent or `#FBF7EE`, 1.5px border `#A47551`, text `#5A3E2B`.
- **Ghost / Text:** Transparent background, `#5A3E2B` text, soft hover tint of `rgba(164, 117, 81, 0.08)`.

### Cards & Book Shelves
- **Book Card:** Surface `#FBF7EE`, 16px rounded corners, 1px border `#D9C7A8`. Includes cover display (proportional 2:3 aspect ratio), book title in bold `#2E1F16`, author in `#7A6250`, and star rating row.
- **Club Discussion Card:** Features member avatar on the right, username, timestamp on the left, comment excerpt, and book reference tag pill.

### Chips & Tags
- **Filter Chips:** 8px rounded corners, `#F5EEDF` background with `#D9C7A8` border. Selected state transitions to `#5A3E2B` fill with `#FBF7EE` typography.
- **Reading Status Badges:** (e.g., "أقرأ حالياً" - Currently Reading, "تمت قراءته" - Finished). Soft caramel tint `#A47551` with 10% opacity background and `#5A3E2B` text.

### Inputs & Search Bars
- **Text Inputs:** Height 48px, 12px rounded corners, background `#FBF7EE`, 1px solid border `#D9C7A8`.
- Focus state shifts border to `#A47551` with a subtle outer ring (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(164, 117, 81, 0.15)`).
- Search icon positioned on the far right; clear icon on the far left.

### Star Ratings & Quotes
- **Stars:** Filled with `#C08A3E` (Caramel Gold), outlined with `#D9C7A8` when unrated. Size 16px-20px with 4px gap.
- **Literary Pull Quotes:** Right-aligned border stripe in `#A47551` (3px width), indented 16px, text rendered in `body-lg` italic naskh.

### Navigation & App Bar
- **Bottom Navigation Bar:** Mobile docked bar using `#FBF7EE` with a 1px top border `#D9C7A8`. Active tab icon and label highlighted in `#5A3E2B`, inactive tabs in `#7A6250`.
- **Top App Bar:** Minimalist beige surface with right-aligned page title and left-aligned context actions (e.g., share, bookmarks).
